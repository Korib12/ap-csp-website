How to get a reading

1
Set the frequency. Drag the slider to whatever frequency locked best in the hardware test. Tone lock should read tone in green before you expect anything else to work.
2
Position the phone. Prop it facing your chest, roughly 20–40 cm away (a book stand or leaning against something works). Screen can face you so you can watch.
3
Hold very still and breathe normally. The scope needs ~30–40 seconds to fill its buffer before it can estimate a rate — watch "buffer filled" climb. Then breathe steadily; the green wave should start to undulate with your breath.
4
Check it tracks you. The real test: breathe faster for a bit, then slower. The BPM number should follow. If it tracks your actual breathing, the engine works and we build the app around it.
5
Test the pattern detector. Breathe normally for 2 minutes: it should say Steady. Then tap Clear and repeat this 4 times: breaths get gradually deeper for about 20 s, gradually shallower for about 20 s, then pause for 10 s. After about 3 minutes it should say Waxing-and-waning pattern with a cycle near 50 s. Keep the deep breaths moderate so you don't get lightheaded.

Honest expectations: this is the hard part. If the green wave is just noise and the number won't settle, try a lower/higher frequency, raise the volume, get closer, and kill any fans/AC. If it still won't lock onto breath after a real attempt, that's useful data — it tells us to adjust the method or lean on the fallback, and we'd rather know now.
