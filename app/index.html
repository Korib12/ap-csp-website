SIMULATION: fake breathing, no microphone

Tonight

Echo listens for breathing with sound you can't hear. No camera and nothing to wear.

  1. Plug in the phone. Echo keeps the screen on all night.
  2. Prop it on the bed, next to the pillow, about 30 cm from the chest, with the bottom edge pointing at the person.
  3. Turn the phone's volume up and take out any earbuds.
  4. Tap Start. Echo tunes itself in a few seconds, then watches quietly.
